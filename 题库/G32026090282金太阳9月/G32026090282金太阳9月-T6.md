## 题目
若 $\cos \left(\alpha + \frac{\pi}{3}\right) + \cos \alpha = 1$，则 $\cos \left(2\alpha + \frac{\pi}{3}\right) =$ [选]

## 选项
A．$-\dfrac{1}{3}$
B．$\dfrac{1}{3}$
C．$-\dfrac{2}{3}$
D．$\dfrac{2}{3}$

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注

## 答案
A

## 分析
本题考查三角恒等变换,考查数学运算的核心素养.

## 解析
(方法一)由 $\cos \left(\alpha +\dfrac{\pi}{3}\right) + \cos \alpha = \dfrac{1}{2}\cos \alpha -\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\sin \alpha +\cos \alpha = \dfrac{3}{2}\cos \alpha -\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\sin \alpha = \sqrt{3}\cos \left(\alpha +\dfrac{\pi}{6}\right) = 1$，得 $\cos \left(\alpha +\dfrac{\pi}{6}\right) = \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{3}$，则 $\cos \left(2\alpha +\dfrac{\pi}{3}\right) = 2\cos^2\left(\alpha +\dfrac{\pi}{6}\right) - 1 = -\dfrac{1}{3}$。

(方法二)由 $\cos\left(\alpha+\dfrac{\pi}{3}\right)+\cos\alpha=2\cos\dfrac{\alpha+\frac{\pi}{3}+\alpha}{2}\cos\dfrac{\alpha+\frac{\pi}{3}-\alpha}{2}=2\cos\left(\alpha+\dfrac{\pi}{6}\right)\cos\dfrac{\pi}{6}=1$，得 $\cos\left(\alpha+\dfrac{\pi}{6}\right)=\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{3}$，则 $\cos\left(2\alpha+\dfrac{\pi}{3}\right)=2\cos^{2}\left(\alpha+\dfrac{\pi}{6}\right)-1=-\dfrac{1}{3}$。
